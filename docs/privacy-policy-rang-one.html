Rang One – Privacy Policy / गोपनीयता नीति

Last updated: 17 November 2026. App: Rang One (package app.bharosa.browser). Developer: Sonu Kumar, India. Contact: sonukumarbank9876@gmail.com

What we collect / हम क्या जानकारी लेते हैं

  • Microphone (आवाज़): Used only when you tap the 🎤 button for voice search, or to record a voiceover in Rang Studio. Audio is converted to text by your phone's speech service or saved on your phone. We do not store your recordings on our servers.
  • Storage (फ़ाइलें): Used only to save videos and files you create or export in Rang Studio, on your own phone.
  • Browsing history (हिस्ट्री): Stays only on your phone. We do not upload it.
  • Rang ID / Rang Mail: If you create a Rang ID or Rang Mail address, we store your email address, display name and mail messages to provide the service. Passwords are never stored on the phone; login tokens are kept in the phone's secure Keystore.
  • Search and AI questions: Text you search or ask Bharosa AI is sent over the internet to get results and answers. Websites you open follow their own privacy policies.

What we do not do / हम क्या नहीं करते

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not show third-party ads; the built-in ad-block is on by default.
  • We do not access contacts, SMS, camera or location.

Children

Rang One is not directed at children under 13.

Delete your data / डेटा हटाएँ

Uninstall the app to remove all data on your phone. To delete your Rang ID or Rang Mail data, email sonukumarbank9876@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy; the new date will be shown above.
