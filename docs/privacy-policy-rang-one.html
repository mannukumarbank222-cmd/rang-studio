Rang One – Privacy Policy / गोपनीयता नीति

Last updated: 20 November 2026 (app version 4.7). App: Rang One – web browser (package app.bharosa.browser).
Developer: PlayRang Technologies, Begusarai, Bihar, India. Contact: mannukumarbank222@gmail.com

Summary / सारांश

Rang One is a web browser. It has no account, no login, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.
Rang One एक वेब ब्राउज़र है। इसमें कोई अकाउंट, लॉगिन, विज्ञापन या एनालिटिक्स नहीं है। हम आपका निजी डेटा न इकट्ठा करते हैं, न बेचते हैं, न किसी से साझा करते हैं।

Data stored only on your phone / सिर्फ़ आपके फ़ोन में रखा डेटा

  • Bookmarks, history, speed-dial shortcuts, downloads list and offline pages are saved only inside the app on your phone. They are never uploaded to us. (बुकमार्क, हिस्ट्री, शॉर्टकट, डाउनलोड सूची और ऑफ़लाइन पेज सिर्फ़ आपके फ़ोन में सेव होते हैं।)
  • Cookies and website data are kept by Android System WebView on your phone so websites keep you signed in, like any browser. Private tabs do not save history, do not store cookies while open, and clear the cache when closed.
  • Downloads are saved by Android's Download Manager in your phone's Downloads folder.
  • You can delete all of this any time: Menu → Settings → Clear browsing data, or by uninstalling the app.

What leaves your phone / फ़ोन से बाहर क्या जाता है

  • Text you type in the address bar is sent directly to the search engine you choose (Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo), and pages you open are loaded from those websites. Each website and search engine follows its own privacy policy. Rang One does not route your browsing through our servers.

Permissions / अनुमतियाँ

  • Internet – to load web pages.
  • Network state – to show a message and your offline pages when there is no internet.

Rang One does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts, SMS, phone or storage permissions. Website requests for camera, microphone or location are refused.

Children / बच्चे

Rang One is a general-purpose browser and is not directed at children under 13.
